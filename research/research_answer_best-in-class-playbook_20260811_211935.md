## Candidate-side must-wins

1. **High-signal profile in under 10 minutes**
   - Import rÃ©sumÃ©, LinkedIn, Behance, Dribbble, personal site, GitHub, or showreel.
   - AI extracts skills but requires candidate confirmation; distinguish **claimed**, **demonstrated**, and **verified** skills.
   - Preserve creative work context: problem, role, constraints, contribution, measurable result, and collaboration.
   - This is critical because Behance and Dribbble are strong discovery/showcase destinations but are not necessarily complete hiring workflows.  
     Sources: https://www.behance.net/  
     https://dribbble.com/

2. **Transparent, useful discovery**
   - Default cards should show salary/range, employment type, work mode, exact area, commute time, visa/relocation support, freshness, employer verification, and expected response time.
   - Salary visibility deserves priority: a cited job-search UX review reports **67% of job seekers actively look for salary information**. Validate this locally.  
     Source: https://traveltime.com/blog/job-portals-top-10-search-ux-examples
   - Map is a decision tool, not decoration: support commute-time isochrones, transport mode, hybrid-day assumptions, and cluster exploration. Commute-time search reduces the need to cross-check Google Maps and can avoid rejecting roles that are farther by distance but easier to reach.  
     Source: https://traveltime.com/blog/job-portals-top-10-search-ux-examples

3. **Low-friction, high-quality application**
   - One reusable profile; role-specific evidence selection; one-click apply only where the employer has committed to reviewing applications.
   - Let candidates answer âwhy this role?â with AI assistance while preserving their voice.
   - Show exactly what gets shared and allow withdrawal/deletion.

4. **AI companion that improves outcomes, not screen time**
   - Recommend a small set of high-fit roles rather than an infinite feed.
   - Explain fit, identify missing evidence, tailor a portfolio narrative, rehearse interviews, and suggest a realistic salary/commute trade-off.
   - Never fabricate experience, auto-apply indiscriminately, or hide uncertainty.
   - Track acceptance/edit rates and downstream interview outcomes, not chatbot sessions.

5. **Trust and control**
   - Flag suspicious employers, requests for money, unrealistic offers, rÃ©sumÃ© harvesting, and unpaid speculative work.
   - Bayt itself publishes guidance on fake Gulf job offers, demonstrating that authenticity is a category-wide problem rather than a niche concern.

   - FTC guidance recommends independently contacting companies through known legitimate channels when recruiters may be impersonated. WizJobs should operationalize this through domain, company, recruiter, and posting verification.  
     Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/08/job-scams

1. **Create a complete, credible brief quickly**

3. **Shortlist on evidence rather than keyword volume**

4. **Reduce coordination and time-to-hire**

6. **Offer a better creative assessment**

   - Avoid AI-only ranking and avoid unpaid production work disguised as assessment.

## Definition of âbestâ

Use a **qualified-hire north-star**, not MAU, impressions, or chatbot usage:

> **Qualified match rate:** percentage of employer shortlists that produce a mutually confirmed interview or work sample, and percentage of those processes producing an accepted offer or paid engagement within a defined period.

Track four linked scorecards:

**Candidate value**
- Median time from onboarding to first relevant opportunity.
- Search-to-qualified-application rate.
- Application completion rate and repeat application rate.
- Interview rate per application.
- Candidate-reported match quality and trust.
- Percentage of active profiles with at least one verified/evidenced project.
- Commute-filter usage and reduction in irrelevant job views.
- 30/90-day candidate retention and offer acceptance.

**Employer value**
- Brief completion rate and time to publish.
- Qualified applicants per roleânot raw applications.
- Time from publish to first qualified shortlist.
- Shortlist-to-interview, interview-to-offer, and offer-acceptance rates.
- Time-to-fill and cost per qualified candidate/hire.
- Employer repeat-posting and subscription renewal.
- Response-SLA adherence and candidate satisfaction.

**Marketplace liquidity**
- Weekly active candidates and employers in the launch wedge.
- **Role-level fill rate:** roles receiving at least three qualified candidates within seven days.
- Median time to first qualified match.
- Search-to-contact and contact-to-response rates.
- Supply-demand ratio by role, seniority, location, salary, and work mode.
- Percentage of active listings verified, fresh, salary-transparent, and responsive.
- Concentration risk: share of activity from the top employers/candidates.

**Trust and AI quality**
- Fake/spam reports per 1,000 listings; confirmed fraud rate.
- Stale-listing rate and employer removal/expiry compliance.
- Candidate consent, correction, deletion, and appeal rates.
- AI recommendation acceptance/edit rate.
- Match precision at top 5/10, interview uplift versus non-AI baseline, and false-positive/false-negative rates.
- Selection-rate and interview-rate disparities across protected or proxy attributes; do not use sensitive characteristics for ranking.
- Human override frequency and override outcome quality.

## Principal risks and mitigations

- **Cold start / empty map:** launch city-and-role pods; concierge-match supply and demand; seed verified inventory; never show a visually rich map with low-quality listings.
- **Map novelty without hiring value:** measure qualified actions and commute decisions; make list view equally strong; map only wins if it improves discovery, fit, or conversion.
- **AI hallucination, bias, and over-automation:** retrieval from supplied evidence, confidence labels, candidate confirmation, explainability, audit logs, human approval, bias testing, and appeal.
- **Portfolio plagiarism or AI-generated work:** provenance fields, links, live walkthroughs, structured work samples, and clear distinction between AI-assisted and independently produced work.
- **Fake jobs, recruiter spam, and data harvesting:** business-domain verification, recruiter identity checks, manual review for risk signals, rate limits, reporting, rapid takedown, and visible employer responsiveness.
- **Low-quality employer demand:** require compensation and process transparency; score employers on response behavior; penalize ghosting and stale postings.
- **Overly broad positioning:** focus initially on a few creative-tech categories and Dubai clusters; expand only after repeat hiring is demonstrated.
- **Privacy and UAE compliance:** explicit consent for rÃ©sumÃ©/portfolio processing, data minimization, deletion/export, access controls, documented AI decisioning, and legal review of employment, visa, and cross-border data flows.
- **Metric gaming:** optimize for interviews, accepted offers, paid engagements, retention, and candidate trustânot applications, clicks, map interactions, or chatbot sessions.
