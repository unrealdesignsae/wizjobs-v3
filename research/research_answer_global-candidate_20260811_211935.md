### 9. Recommended WizJobs product principles

- **Position daily matches as a curated shortlist, not a search-results dump.**
- **Make every recommendation auditable:** show match reasons, gaps, confidence, and controls.
- **Treat salary, location, authorization, schedule, and remote policy as hard constraints where appropriate.**
