Risks and anti-goals
10 / 12
Protect the wedge

The fastest way to lose is to optimize activity over progress.

Anti-goals

  • Raw listing volume and uncontrolled aggregation
  • One-click or automated mass apply
  • Match percentages presented as hire odds
  • Social feed, public reviews, or ad-auction relevance
  • Black-box ranking, personality scoring, or reusable AI interviews

Guardrails

  • Authorized supply with canonical IDs and close events
  • Intent checks plus employer review obligations
  • Evidence, unknowns, confidence, and user control
  • Core flows work without Wizy
  • Stop segments that cannot deliver five credible matches
