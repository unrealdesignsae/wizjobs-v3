# WizJobs research presentation

A self-contained HTML presentation for the 11 August 2026 general-audience research readout.

The supplied Wizjobs logo is stored at `assets/wizjobs-logo.png` and used verbatim in the title and slide headers.

## Open

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. An internet connection is required for Plus Jakarta Sans and Chart.js. Press `F` to enter fullscreen.

## Shortcuts

- Right arrow, Space, Page Down: next slide
- Left arrow, Page Up: previous slide
- Home / End: first / last slide
- F: fullscreen
- Click the slide background: next slide

## Charts

1. UAE workforce context: verified 2024 sector shares from FCSC. The small residual category is calculated from the published shares.
2. Competitor map: directional research synthesis, not measured market scores.
3. Winner versus gap comparison: directional emphasis derived from the canonical competitor review, not percentages.

## Design system

`design-tokens.css` documents the WizJobs palette, Plus Jakarta Sans type scale, 4px spacing rhythm, radius system, restrained shadow, and motion timing. The visual language adapts Airbnb DLS principles of unity, universality, iconicity, restraint, and content-led depth while using WizJobs navy and blue throughout.
